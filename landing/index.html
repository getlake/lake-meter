A capability of Lake

Meter is cost control for your AI factory.

Meter is a capability of Lake, not a separate product. It puts a cost on every step of the agent's plan, holds a cap at run time, and can require a budget owner above a limit you set.

Start free The Lake platform
  • Every step of the plan gets a complexity rating, a recommended model and a $ range.
  • A per-run cap stops and asks before the agent spends more. In unattended runs, it stops the turn.
  • Above a limit you set, a budget owner must also approve that exact plan version.
  • Reviewers can suggest a cheaper model, a dropped step or a tighter cap, in the same review as the plan.

Meter ships with Lake. The install is on the pricing section and in the setup guide. Existing Meter-only installs keep working.

Lake is one product. Meter (cost estimates, model picks, and run caps) is free forever with no limit. Gate, the plan-approval capability, is free for up to 3 users and 20 plan reviews/month in the same install.
